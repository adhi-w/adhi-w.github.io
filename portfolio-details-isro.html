The work

I worked on an intelligent search and rescue robot designed to negotiate difficult terrain. Independent leg movements supported stair climbing, while mapping and obstacle-avoidance methods helped the robot interpret its surroundings.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Investigated independent leg motion for stairs and uneven surfaces.
  • Worked with SLAM and fuzzy-logic obstacle avoidance.
  • Evaluated behavior in simulation and physical robot experiments.
