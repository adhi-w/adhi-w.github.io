The work

Our team designed and manufactured a four-degree-of-freedom robotic arm for grasping and moving objects. With no CNC machine available, we fabricated components using lathes, drills, and mills.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Developed the mechanical structure around kinematics and actuation requirements.
  • Manufactured and assembled parts using conventional workshop equipment.
  • Demonstrated grasping and object transfer with items such as a bottle and tennis ball.
