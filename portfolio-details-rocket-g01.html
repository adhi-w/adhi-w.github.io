The work

Our team developed a meteorology payload with onboard sensors, actuators, and a ground-station monitoring interface. The project brought hardware integration, launch preparation, and flight-data analysis into one competition system.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Integrated onboard measurements and live telemetry to the ground station.
  • Carried out vibration, acceleration, and homing-related tests.
  • The team won first place at the Indonesian National Rocket Competition, KORINDO 2010.
