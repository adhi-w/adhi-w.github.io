The work

I contributed to the mechanical design of HuroEvolutionTN. The design used an aluminum body and parallel leg mechanisms to maintain the relationship between the hip and foot planes while supporting stable locomotion.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Worked on a 15-degree-of-freedom structure and mechanical integration.
  • Used leg springs to reduce actuator load and support the resting posture.
  • Contributed to locomotion work combining a linear inverted pendulum model with PD control.
