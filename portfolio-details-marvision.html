The work

For my bachelor’s project, I developed a vision-based control system for two holonomic soccer robots. An overhead camera detected robots and the ball, while a leader–follower strategy selected actions based on each robot’s distance from the ball.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Implemented image segmentation, marker recognition, and ball detection.
  • Built calibration and tracking interfaces for the vision system.
  • Coordinated robot movement to find the ball and dribble toward the goal.
