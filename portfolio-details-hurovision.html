The work

I developed parts of the vision system for HuroEvolutionTN, including color-based object detection and vision-based localization. The work addressed changing illumination and camera movement during robot soccer.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Applied hue and saturation processing and histogram-based regression for color detection.
  • Used Kalman filtering to predict ball movement.
  • Connected visual information with task-oriented behavior and decision-making.
