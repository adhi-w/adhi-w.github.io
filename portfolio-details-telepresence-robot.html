The work

I helped build a telepresence system that lets people experience a remote location through a mobile robot and a VR headset. Working with software, hardware, and design colleagues, I brought a simulated research setup into a physical robot study. The study compared unwound camera rotations with conventional coupled rotations to investigate user comfort.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Integrated 360° camera footage with robot orientation in Unity and ROS 2.
  • Worked on video synchronization, communication latency, and image vibration.
  • Deployed the robot, mapped the environment, and supported user studies.
