Research experience

November 2023–present

Doctoral Researcher · Cloud-XR & Gaming

University of Helsinki · Department of Computer Science, Finland

Research alongside PhD studies in Computer Science. My work focuses on remote rendering, foveated streaming, neural video codecs, and visual quality.

  • Implement gaze-adaptive spatial compression in ALVR, connecting server-side HLSL foveation with client-side Vulkan reconstruction, eye tracking, and H.265 video streaming.
  • Develop latency compensation through gaze prediction, hysteresis-based stabilization, and adaptive foveal sizing, with synchronized parameters across the server and headset.
  • Build Python workflows for gaze analysis, parameter tuning, and visual-quality evaluation using eye-weighted PSNR and FovVideoVDP.
  • Investigate foveated neural video compression for VR. This work is ongoing and unpublished.
  • Develop research software in C++ and Python to support video-system experiments.
  • Contribute to user studies, undergraduate thesis supervision, and teaching; collaborate with researchers at Aalto University and National Tsing Hua University.

Selected project responsibilities

Gaze-adaptive foveation · ACM Multimedia 2025

First-author paper comparing three dynamic foveation methods in an end-to-end VR system. The evaluated implementation supported native 72 FPS operation and a complementary 16-participant study.

Latency-driven optimization · ACM Multimedia 2026

First-author paper reporting 24.04% lower mean gaze error in the evaluated conditions, with significant visual and overall quality gains in a 30-participant study. Conference: 10–14 November 2026.

Neural video compression · Ongoing

Explore foveated neural video compression for VR through ongoing, unpublished research.

Researcher · Immersive Telepresence

University of Oulu · Center for Ubiquitous Computing, Finland

Research integrating virtual reality and mobile robotics. The work combined immersive software, sensing, system integration, and studies of user comfort and experience.

Selected project responsibilities

XR cinema rendering · 2019–2021

Developed mesh generation and image-mapping software for panoramic media, working with UV coordinates, shaders, equirectangular and cube-map projections, and interpolation. Rendered 16K still images and 8K video.

Headset and streaming integration · 2021–2023

Integrated Oculus Rift S, Quest 2, and Varjo headsets with XR cinema and telepresence playback. Worked with eye tracking, video passthrough, and live video to connect immersive applications with different devices.

Immersive perception experiments · 2022–2023

Adapted rendering software for perceptual experiments, produced panoramic media, and implemented gaze tracking and event handling. Supported VR studies and analysis of user experience.

Robotic telepresence · 2021–2022

Integrated VR with a mobile robot, environment mapping, navigation, and obstacle avoidance. Supported deployments and user studies to assess comfort and the remote experience.

Headset benchmarking · 2019–2020

Worked with OptoFidelity engineers on robot-based headset evaluation, including resolution, motion-to-photon latency, field of view, and tracking accuracy. Built Python tools for analysis and visualization.

Oulu campus virtual environment · 2020–2021

Created building models, PBR materials, lighting, avatars, and animations. Programmed interactive objects, optimized rendering performance, and connected the Unity environment to ROS 2 through TCP/IP.

Panoramic media · 2019–2022

Captured and processed high-resolution 360-degree images and videos for robotic telepresence and immersive meetings, working on stitching quality and playback integration.

Photogrammetry and 3D scanning · 2019–2022

Supported scanner development, capture planning, equipment operation, and scan processing. Produced assets for human avatars, virtual museum content, and 3D printing.

Inertial sensing · 2019–2020

Developed an IMU software library for a mobile robot, integrated it with camera and sensing systems, and tested it in laboratory and field settings.
