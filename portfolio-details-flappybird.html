The work

I developed a mechanical bird prototype powered by the wind. A propeller drives the gear mechanism to move the wings, while the body rotates to face the wind.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Designed the relationship between propeller, gears, and flapping wings.
  • Developed the body, wing, and tail components.
  • Assembled a prototype combining mechanical function with an expressive physical form.
