The work

During my internship at PT. Djaja Sakti Sari, I worked with engineers and technicians on a slitting machine for recovering tobacco from rejected products. The design combined a three-phase AC motor with vibration-based separation.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Contributed to machine design, fabrication, and testing.
  • Worked on mechanisms for slitting and material separation.
  • Gained practical experience with production equipment and maintenance.
