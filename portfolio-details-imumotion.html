The work

I evaluated IMU-based motion tracking using an arm manipulator with reference measurements from encoders and optical motion capture. The setup made it possible to compare tracked joint motion and the reconstructed end-effector trajectory.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Mounted IMUs on a manipulator to record shoulder and elbow motion.
  • Used forward kinematics to reconstruct the end-effector position.
  • Compared circular motion trajectories and reference measurements to assess tracking performance.
