The work

I developed three iterations of a control board for the multi-agent robot soccer platform. The electronics brought together motor drivers, power regulation, wireless communication, and debugging connections.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Created schematics, board layouts, and component wiring designs.
  • Considered motor current demand, power consumption, and voltage drop.
  • Built transmitter and receiver electronics to connect a PC with the robots.
