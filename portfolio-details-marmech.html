The work

I designed and built a compact holonomic robot platform for multi-agent soccer. The mechanical layout had to fit the drive system, ball-handling hardware, electronics, and battery into a small body.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Developed three versions of the mechanical design.
  • Integrated four omni-wheel motors, a dribbler motor, and a solenoid kicking mechanism.
  • Fabricated and assembled acrylic and aluminum components.
