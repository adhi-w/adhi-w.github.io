The work

I was part of the team developing HuroEvolutionTN, a teen-size biped humanoid robot for international competitions. My work at Taiwan Tech spanned mechanical and electronic development, vision software, and task-oriented robot behavior.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Contributed to the integration of robot hardware, perception, and control.
  • Developed behavior and decision-making software for competition tasks.
  • The team placed first in the RoboCup 2015 Teen-size Technical Challenge and second in Teen-size Soccer.
