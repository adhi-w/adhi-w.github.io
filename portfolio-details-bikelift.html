The work

For a course project, I designed and manufactured a hydraulic bike lift using steel hollow sections and bars. The work combined structural design with hands-on welding, drilling, and assembly.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Designed the lifting mechanism and structural components.
  • Prepared engineering drawings and bills of materials.
  • Fabricated and assembled the lift for maintenance access.
