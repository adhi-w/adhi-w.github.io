The work

Following the G-01 project, our team developed a new payload for KOMURINDO 2011. It combined a camera with compass, acceleration, pressure, and humidity sensing, alongside a homing actuator system.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Integrated sensors, imaging, and telemetry in the payload.
  • Monitored transmitted data during the launch and analyzed recorded measurements.
  • The team won first place at the Indonesian National Payload Rocket Competition, KOMURINDO 2011.
