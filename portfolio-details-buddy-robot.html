The work

I worked with Optofidelity engineers to evaluate software and test procedures for VR headset benchmarking. The project used the Buddy robot platform to investigate display and tracking performance under controlled conditions.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Supported tests of resolution, motion-to-photon latency, field of view, and tracking accuracy.
  • Evaluated testing procedures with Optofidelity engineers.
  • Developed and maintained Python tools for analysis and visualization.
