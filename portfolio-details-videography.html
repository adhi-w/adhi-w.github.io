The work

I captured and produced 360° images and video for research in remote presence. The work included camera setups on mobile robots and immersive meeting scenarios, with attention to stitching quality and the viewing experience in a headset.

Project contributions & outcomes

  • Worked with different panoramic camera systems and recording configurations.
  • Produced video sequences for robot telepresence and remote meetings.
  • Prepared high-resolution footage and addressed visible stitching artifacts.
